# Dream

Interactive 3D pieces that run in the browser, published with GitHub Pages at
https://brigham4210.github.io/Dream/

- **[Dream Garage](https://brigham4210.github.io/Dream/Garage/)**: a 3D showroom holding a 1977 Leyland Mini 1000 Mk IV and a 1966 Chevrolet C10 stepside. Drag to walk around, scroll to zoom, click doors, hoods, boots and tailgates to open them.
- **[My Closet](https://brigham4210.github.io/Dream/Closet/)**: a walk-in closet with 18 pieces of clothing hanging on three walls. Drag to look around, scroll to zoom, click a piece to take it down. The list lives in `Closet/clothes.js`, one line per piece.
- **[My Music Room](https://brigham4210.github.io/Dream/Music/)**: a grand piano, an acoustic guitar, an electric guitar and an electric bass on the wall, a drum kit, a cajón, a singing studio, and two video cameras on tripods (one on the piano, one in the studio). Click the piano keys, the strings, the drums, the cymbals or the cajón to play them (the sound is made in the browser), the piano lid to lift it, the booth door to open it, the microphone to switch the recording light, and either camera to roll and see its shot on the monitor.
- **[My Dream Kitchen](https://brigham4210.github.io/Dream/Kitchen/)**: an empty kitchen, ready to cook in, with no food anywhere. Light wood cabinets under marble with a sink, a fridge and a pantry; a clear marble island with three stools under brass pendants; a stand mixer, a blender, an electric crêpe maker, a rice cooker and a built-in microwave; a six-burner gas range carrying a wok, bamboo steamers, a palayok, a skillet, a crêpe pan and a pot; a Korean barbecue and hotpot table under two copper extractor ducts; a wood-fired pizza oven; a comal (steel flat-top); copper pans over a marble sideboard; and a clock that keeps the real time. Click doors, drawers, knobs, the tap, lamps and appliances, toss the wok, light the grill or the pizza oven, ring a pan (the sounds are made in the browser). Each thing is one file in `Kitchen/`.
- **[My Workout Room](https://brigham4210.github.io/Dream/Gym/)**: a back wall of mirrors that really reflect the room, with a double beech dance barre standing in front of them; a treadmill facing the mirrors; a power rack with a loaded barbell, spotter arms, a pull-up bar, spare plates and a lat pulldown whose cable runs over two pulleys to a weight stack; and free weights: ten pairs of hex dumbbells on a rack, three kettlebells and a flat bench. Click the treadmill to start or stop the belt (the console counts time and distance), the barbell to press it, the lat bar to pull the stack up, a dumbbell or kettlebell to lift it (the sounds are made in the browser). Each thing is one file in `Gym/`.

Everything is static HTML and JavaScript; three.js is loaded from a CDN, so an internet connection is needed.
