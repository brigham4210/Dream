# Dream

Interactive 3D pieces that run in the browser, published with GitHub Pages at
https://brigham4210.github.io/Dream/

- **[Dream Garage](https://brigham4210.github.io/Dream/Garage/)**: a 3D showroom holding a 1977 Leyland Mini 1000 Mk IV and a 1966 Chevrolet C10 stepside. Drag to walk around, scroll to zoom, click doors, hoods, boots and tailgates to open them.
- **[My Closet](https://brigham4210.github.io/Dream/Closet/)**: a walk-in closet with 18 pieces of clothing hanging on three walls. Drag to look around, scroll to zoom, click a piece to take it down. The list lives in `Closet/clothes.js`, one line per piece.
- **[My Music Room](https://brigham4210.github.io/Dream/Music/)**: a grand piano, an acoustic guitar, a singing studio and a video camera on a tripod. Click the piano keys or the guitar strings to play them (the sound is made in the browser), the piano lid to lift it, the booth door to open it the microphone to switch the recording light, and the camera to roll and see its shot on the monitor.

Everything is static HTML and JavaScript; three.js is loaded from a CDN, so an internet connection is needed.
